Cranberry Hollow: The Great Drumstick Dash is a third-person chase. You are Tom Wattles, a bronze turkey, and Farmer Hank is right behind you with a net. The run starts in the farmyard, cuts through the barn and porch, and ends in the kitchen, where the oven breathes fire and a carving knife slams the lane. Jump, slide, dash, and hide. Reach the woods, or become dinner.

## Controls

- A / D or Left / Right: change lane
- Space or W or Up: jump
- S or Down: slide
- Shift: dash
- E or H: hide in hay, corn, or a cabinet
- Q: feather decoy
- P: pause
- M: mute
- R or Enter: restart

## Stages

1. Farmyard. Fences, hay bales, pumpkins, corn, and the red barn.
2. Barn and porch. Stalls, barrels, lanterns, and chickens that give you away.
3. Kitchen. Checkerboard floor, a bursting oven, and the carving knife.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://127.0.0.1:8080. Another option: `npx serve game` or `python -m http.server -d game 8080`, then open http://localhost:8080.
